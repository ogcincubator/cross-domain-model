## SHACL shapes

Two complementary SHACL shape sources are declared (`shaclShapes` in `bblock.json`):

- [CDO-Shapes-PROV-O](https://github.com/Cyber-Domain-Ontology/CDO-Shapes-PROV-O) — a
  canonical, community-maintained shapes library (Apache-2.0). It covers the
  `owl:disjointWith` class pairs and other curated consistency checks around
  `prov:Influence` and its subclasses.
- `shapes.shacl` (this directory) — mechanically derived from PROV-O's own
  `rdfs:domain`/`rdfs:range` axioms, giving `sh:class`/`sh:datatype`/cardinality
  shapes for every class and property PROV-O defines. Generated by
  `generate-shapes.py` in this directory; see that script's docstring for the
  full rationale (it follows the conversion rule categories documented by
  [sparna-git/owl2shacl](https://github.com/sparna-git/owl2shacl), reimplemented
  directly against PROV-O since owl2shacl's own ruleset depends on a
  TopBraid-only SPARQL extension function). Re-run the script rather than
  hand-editing its output.
